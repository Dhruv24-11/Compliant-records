21 CFR Part 11 & GAMP 5 Regulatory Mapping

21 CFR §11.10(a)

System Validation & Immutability

Records are permanently locked upon signature; subsequent modifications are strictly rejected across all roles.

21 CFR §11.10(e)

Secure, Time-Stamped Audit Trails

Append-only table capturing user, action, field changed, old value, new value, and timestamp. No edit or delete routes exist.

21 CFR §11.10(g) & (d)

Authority Checks & RBAC

Segregation of duties: Operators create/edit; Reviewers sign; Admins manage users.

21 CFR §11.50

Signature Manifestations

Signed electronic records clearly display signer name, role, date/time, and meaning (Reviewed, Approved, Rejected).

21 CFR §11.200

Signature Components & Controls

Re-authenticates password against bcrypt hash before recording electronic signature.
